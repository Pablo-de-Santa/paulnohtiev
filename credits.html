Asset credits

The space sequence uses the planetary maps credited below. The current pixel-world aircraft, clouds, forest, mountains, cabin, furniture, pond, character, Odie and animations are original geometry authored for this portfolio. Paul and Odie’s appearances use personal photos supplied by Paul as references.

Pixel clearing and sound

The seasonal landscape, day/night sky, aurora, code display and interactions are created with Three.js. Ambient music and environmental effects are synthesized with Web Audio, except Odie’s real bark credited below; no Minecraft music, recordings or assets are used. The characters are original stylized models, not Apple assets.

Odie’s bark

Tiny Dog Barking in Park by qubodup, CC0. A short excerpt was trimmed, faded and converted to mono for Odie’s Speak action. Paul’s robot-like voice is originally synthesized; no Stray audio is used.

Earlier mountain-valley assets

These assets remain bundled as authoring references for earlier studies; they are not the scenery used in the current pixel clearing. Their original credits are retained.

  • Mountainside by Dario Barresi and Rico Cilliers, Poly Haven, CC0. Rock scans and surface maps; geometry simplified and textures compressed. The surrounding mountain terrain is authored using these surface maps.
  • Pine Sapling Small by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, Poly Haven, CC0. Simplified, resized and instanced.
  • House with Thatched Roof by Venturon, CC0. Recolored and arranged as a small village.
  • Animated bird by Mirada, distributed with the Three.js examples. Recolored and scaled for distant silhouettes. Three.js MIT license.

Planetary maps

Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth day and night maps, Earth cloud mask, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune: Solar System Scope / INOVE, based on NASA imagery and elevation data. CC BY 4.0. Original JPGs are bundled unchanged; applied to lit sphere meshes with additional atmosphere shading. These are visualization maps, not measurements.

Bundled aircraft from earlier versions

  • Boeing 747 by 1883, downloaded via MediaWiki3D. CC BY 4.0.
  • Boeing 787-9 by Nobilis 2. CC BY 4.0.
  • Private Jet by Nick the Name. CC BY 4.0.
  • Cessna 172 by e737. CC BY 4.0.

The 787, private jet, and Cessna are the optimized/repackaged GLBs distributed by God’s Eye View: transforms baked, models centered, with texture and geometry optimizations documented there. This portfolio additionally normalizes display scale and orientation at runtime and animates the models. The 747, earlier 787, private jet and Cessna remain bundled but are not loaded in the current sequence. These third-party aircraft remain under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement is implied.

The earlier realistic fleet — Boeing 737, Airbus A380, Airbus A320, and Boeing 787 — is by AMV Lab, CC BY 4.0. Downloaded GLBs are bundled locally; display scale and orientation are were normalized in the earlier version. Original files: A380.glb and A320.glb, B737.glb, and B787.glb.

The character, Morkie and Edmonton-inspired city are original stylized geometry, not Apple assets or city photogrammetry.

Tower Defense arcade

The cabinet plays Paul’s Kingdom Under Siege / Tower Defense, bundled locally from commit 10693ca. Its original artwork, gameplay and audio are retained. Camp audio pauses while the arcade is open. The cabinet model and integration are original to this portfolio.
